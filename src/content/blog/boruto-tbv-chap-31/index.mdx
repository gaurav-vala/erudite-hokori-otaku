## Introduction

Chapter 30 of Boruto Two Blue Vortex is released! I am a bit late with this one, but yeah for the sake of monthly consistency, I'll try my best to provide my thoughts on the new chapter!

BTW, this month Boruto community on twitter tried to report all the leakers who leak the chapter only 2,3 or sometimes just a day before official chapter releases, which was exciting to see and well mostly a failed execution. Never the less we keep going, making Boruto experience better!

## A whole lot of mess

Chapter starts where chapter 30 ended, with Kobu on the rooftop and Inojin trying to save him from Mamushi, and this situation is really a mess (in a good way), We have Shikamaru and Sai debating, Koji and Inojin debating and inbetween Mamushi angry on this random dude who's apparently the proxy of the Daimyo.

With TBV I am seeing how good Ikemoto is with handling different aspects of a same conflict, it happens in every arc, there are multiple things happening in multiple locations and at the end everything comes to a single location, Ikemoto Sensei has been really great with this idea.

## Boruto’s Save

Obviously everyone predicted this happening, at the end Boruto saved Kubo from Mamushi. But the way Ikemoto Sensei showed it was really cool. Boruto comes flying and it's shown in a fisheye view something similar like how he got Matsuri swept through Mamushi and absorbed the fire that Mamushi put on Kobu using his Karma. Ikemoto Sensei has been handling this type of moments in a really stylish way that makes sense too.

## Delta and Sarada

These two were the stars of the Chapter, specially the way Sarada used Ohirume (good to see her using it more), I don't remember last time seeing Delta in full action, but yeah she was in full action mode this chapter (reminders of her fight with Naruto).

Sarada on the other side seems to have caught up with her MS, she attracted all Mamushi clones around her to the spheres and squeezed them until their existence was gone! A little detail here is that her MS pattern starts moving when she's going in full throttle with the power.

## Time for Daemon

Idk how many times I have said this but yeah, this time it seems to be happening, Shikamaru’s last option is to get all Mamushi clones to Eida’s location, and deal with them all together! Daemon seems excited as well, although now I don't know how much he'll be able to handle them in terms of the numbers of Mamushis. He can be shown to go in full berserk mode, I don't think it'll go as smoothly and everyone on internet is also speculating that something crazy should be happening too.

## Conclusion

That's a short review since this month I'm already late, but also nowadays I'm really going with the flow when it comes to posts and writing. This arc is going super good with the way Konoha has become a battleground. And now it's come to everyone getting in a single location, if Ikemoto sensei does it correct things might get more fun and interesting! Looking forward to that.
